How nights are scored

Clouds matter most, and low cloud counts extra. Transparency comes from humidity, since damp air scatters light. Seeing uses jet-stream wind at 250 hPa; fast winds aloft blur stars. Wind uses ground gusts that shake a mount. Darkness counts twilight and how bright and high the moon is. Only hours of astronomical darkness count toward the night's score.
